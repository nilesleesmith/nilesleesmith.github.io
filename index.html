Niles Smith

I’m a web developer with a background in finance and operations. I have a strong dislike of doing the boring stuff, so I build to let me have more time for the fun stuff.

I taught myself a little bit of coding to automate repetitive work and found out I really like building tools that solve real problems and give people back their time. I’m especially interested in Free/Libre and Open Source Software and the idea that we can and should make life a little easier.

I’m just building and looking for the next problem worth solving. If you have a problem, let's work together and solve it!

I'm glad you're here. I'm happy to share my projects, a little bit about myself, and some cute pictures of my dog.
